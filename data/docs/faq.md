# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do Lumen bulbs need a hub?
Lumen bulbs connect directly over Wi-Fi and work without a hub. The Lumen Hub is only needed for Zigbee accessories such as the Lumen Motion Sensor and for controlling more than 50 devices.

## Which voice assistants are supported?
Lumen works with Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant and Apple Home through Matter.

## What is the warranty?
All Lumen products have a 2-year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects. Damage from water, drops or power surges is not covered.

## How do I contact support?
Support is available by chat in the Lumen app every day from 8 AM to 8 PM Eastern Time. Customers can also email support@lumenhome.example.

## Can I use Lumen without the app?
Basic on/off works with a normal wall switch. Colours, schedules and scenes need the Lumen app.
