# Employee Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Lumen Home. It was last updated on 1 March 2026.

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at 2 days per month worked.

Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March if they are not used.

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks of paid leave for the secondary caregiver.

Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance, except for sick leave and emergencies.

Contractors and interns are not covered by this policy. Their leave terms are set in their individual contracts.
