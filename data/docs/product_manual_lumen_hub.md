# Lumen Hub Product Manual

The Lumen Hub (model LH-200) connects Zigbee accessories to your home Wi-Fi network and supports up to 150 devices.

## Setup
1. Plug the hub into power with the included USB-C adapter.
2. Open the Lumen app and tap "Add device", then "Lumen Hub".
3. Wait until the status light turns solid green. This takes about 2 minutes.

The hub only connects to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks. 5 GHz networks are not supported.

## Status light
Solid green means connected. Blinking blue means the hub is in pairing mode. Solid red means the hub has lost its internet connection.

## Factory reset
To factory reset the hub, press and hold the reset button on the back for 10 seconds until the light blinks red three times. A reset removes all paired devices.

## Specifications
Power: 5 V USB-C, 2 W typical. Operating temperature: 0 to 40 degrees Celsius. Wireless: Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz and Zigbee 3.0.
