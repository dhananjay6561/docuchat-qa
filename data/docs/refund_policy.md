# Refund Policy

Customers can return any Lumen Home product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

Products must be returned in their original packaging with all accessories. Opened products are accepted if they are undamaged.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse.

Return shipping is free for defective products. For non-defective returns, a return shipping fee of 6.99 USD is deducted from the refund.

Gift cards and items marked "Final Sale" cannot be refunded.

To start a return, customers open the Orders page in the Lumen app, select the order and choose "Return item". A prepaid return label is emailed within 24 hours.
