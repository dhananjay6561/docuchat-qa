# Shipping Policy

Lumen Home ships to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and India.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free for orders over 50 USD. Orders under 50 USD pay a flat 4.99 USD standard shipping fee.

Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 14.99 USD, regardless of order value.

Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time on a business day are dispatched the same day.

Every shipped order gets a tracking link by email and in the Orders page of the Lumen app.

International orders to the United Kingdom and India may be charged import duties on delivery. These duties are paid by the customer.
